About Us

Committed to improving service delivery in our communities

Who We Are

CommUnity Reporta is a community-driven platform built to bridge the gap between residents and local municipalities across South Africa. We understand the daily frustrations of unreported or unresolved service delivery issues, from potholes and broken streetlights to water leaks and electricity faults.

Our mission is simple: make it easy for anyone to report problems, track progress, and stay informed. Whether you are a student, vendor, worker, or homeowner, you deserve a reliable way to have your voice heard and your concerns addressed.

Why We Exist

Too often, communities struggle with slow response times, lack of feedback, and unclear processes when reporting issues. CommUnity Reporta was created to bring transparency, accountability, and efficiency into service delivery systems.

By combining technology with community participation, we empower residents to take an active role in improving their neighborhoods, because real change starts with informed and engaged citizens.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every community in South Africa has access to fast, transparent, and effective service delivery. A future where reporting an issue is simple, tracking it is clear, and solutions are delivered without unnecessary delays.
